## [0.1.0]

First release.

### Added

- Bass preamp/DI with Level, Blend, Drive, Character, Low, Mid, High, Bite and Cab Bypass. Character sweeps a pre-clip mid cut or boost, adds gain above noon and lets hard notes bias the second of two 4x oversampled clipping stages.
- Eight factory presets (Studio Flip, Clean DI, Seventies Stack, Thumpy Funk, Fat Tube, Warm Compression, Modern Crunch, Fuzzed Out), loudness-matched on a bass line, in LV2, VST3 and CLAP.
- KiCad schematic of the modelled circuit, drawn as analogue stand-ins.
