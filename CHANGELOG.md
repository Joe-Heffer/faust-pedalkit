## [0.1.0]

First release: Linux x86_64 plugins in LV2, VST3 and CLAP formats, with an `install.sh` that installs them into `~/.lv2`, `~/.vst3` and `~/.clap`.

### Added

- **BassDrive** 0.1.0: bass overdrive with clean/driven blend and two-band EQ.
- **BassPreamp** 0.1.0: tube-voiced bass preamp/DI with blend, presence, speaker voicing, three-band EQ and factory presets (LV2).
